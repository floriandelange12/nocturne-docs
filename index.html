Setting up Nocturne

Nocturne is built for studios that sell time before they sell things — barbers, tattoo artists, salons, spas and clinics — and that also want a proper shop when they need one. This guide takes a fresh install to a finished storefront in about half an hour. Everything here is done in the theme editor and in your Shopify admin. You never need to touch code.

Before you start

Duplicate your theme before editing any code. Go to Online Store → Themes, open the … menu on Nocturne and choose Duplicate. Edit the copy. Code changes are not covered by theme support, and they are lost when you install a theme update. If you want changes that the settings cannot make, hire a Shopify Partner.

Open Online Store → Themes → Customize. Almost every setting in this guide lives under Theme settings, the gear icon at the bottom of the left sidebar. Section-specific settings live on the sections themselves.

  1. Set your name, tagline and logo under Brand identity.
  2. Fill in Contact — it feeds the footer, the map block, click-to-call and your search listing.
  3. Choose how people book under Booking. Set a phone number as the safety net.
  4. Decide under Store setup whether your site shows services only, or services and products.
  5. Replace the placeholder photography with your own.

Brand identity

Theme settings → Brand identity. Your studio name and tagline appear in the header, in the mobile menu, in the footer and in link previews when someone shares your site. Keep the tagline to two to five words — on a phone it sits under the name on a single line, and a long one is shortened.

Add your logo here too. A wide logo reads better than a tall one; if you have no logo, leave it empty and the theme sets your name in its display serif, which is how the demo looks. The favicon is set in the same place.

Contact & opening hours

Theme settings → Contact. These fields do more than fill the footer. Your address feeds the visit section and the structured data that puts your studio on the map in search results. Your phone number becomes a click-to-call link on mobile and is the last fallback for the booking buttons. Your email is required for link previews.

Opening hours are set per day in the same group and appear in the footer, the visit section and your search listing. Leave a day blank to show it as closed.

Booking

Theme settings → Booking. This is the heart of the theme. Every "Book" button on the site — header, hero, service cards, the floating button on mobile — routes through one setting, so you change it once.

With a booking app

Install your booking app and enable its app embed under Theme settings → App embeds. Then set Booking provider to that app and, if the app supports it, turn on the in-page widget so the booking flow opens over your site instead of sending the customer away. Per discipline you can pass a branch or staff group so each world opens on its own calendar.

With a booking link

No app? Paste your provider's booking page as a full https:// address. Buttons open it in a panel over your site, or in a new tab.

With a phone number

Leave both empty and set a phone number: the buttons become click-to-call. Plenty of studios run this way and it works perfectly.

Always set a phone number, whatever else you use. The buttons fall back in order — widget, link, booking page, phone, email, contact page — so a customer is never left with a button that does nothing, even if your booking app is down.

Disciplines

Theme settings → Disciplines & booking context. A discipline is a world within your studio: barbering and tattooing under one roof, or hair and beauty, or a clinic with two practices. Nocturne runs one to five of them from a single store, with one cart and one checkout.

Set the number of disciplines first — choose one and the switcher disappears entirely. For each one:

SettingWhat it does
LabelThe name on the switcher above the header.
PageWhere the switcher sends people.
URL keywordHow the theme knows which world a visitor is in. Use a word that appears in that world's address and nowhere else — tattoo for /pages/tattoo.
Accent colourRe-tints that world. Text on the accent is adjusted automatically so it stays readable.
Booking contextPassed to your booking app so the right calendar opens.
MenuOptional. A separate navigation menu for that world.

Keep the keywords apart. If two disciplines both match the same address, the theme cannot tell which world a visitor is in.

What your site shows

Theme settings → Store setup, the setting called What this site shows. It has two positions, and it changes what your header and footer carry — never what your site can do.

Services onlyServices and products
Search iconhiddenshown
Cart iconhidden until something is in the cartshown
Account iconshownshown
Payment icons in the footerhiddenshown

On Services only your product pages, collections, search and checkout all keep working exactly as before — they are simply not advertised in the chrome. The cart icon reappears the moment a customer puts something in the cart, so nobody is ever stranded with a basket they cannot reach.

The account icon stays in both positions. Shopify requires it, and in a studio it is also the door to a customer's appointments.

Navigation

Menus are Shopify's, not the theme's: Online Store → Navigation. Build your main menu there and it appears in the header and in the mobile menu.

Until you build one, the header shows a small starter menu so a fresh install is never empty. The moment your menu has a single link, yours takes over. If you run more than one discipline you can give each its own menu under Theme settings → Disciplines & booking context.

The account panel has its own menu. Create one named customer-account-main-menu under Online Store → Navigation and its links appear when a customer opens the account icon. Skip it and Shopify shows Orders and Profile, which is enough for most studios.

Colours, type & presets

Nocturne ships three presets — complete looks you can apply in one click when you install the theme, or rebuild yourself at any time from the settings.

PresetFeels like
NocturneWarm near-black with gold. The signature look: cinematic, evening, appointment-led.
IvoryThe same structure in daylight — paper, ink and a deeper gold. Calm and clinical without being cold.
EmberWarm and retail-forward, with the shop chrome on and a product shelf on the home page.

Colours live under Theme settings → Colors as six schemes. Every section picks a scheme, so you can alternate light and dark down a page without editing colours twice. Contrast is checked as part of the theme's build; if you change a scheme, keep text and background far enough apart to stay readable.

Type is under Theme settings → Typography: a display face for headings and a text face for everything else. Nocturne ships Playfair Display and Work Sans. Both can be swapped for any font Shopify offers.

Sections & pages

Every page is built from sections you can add, reorder and remove. Nocturne carries a full set for a studio: cinematic hero, discipline split, service and price menus, appointment steps, team profiles, before-and-after portfolio, atmosphere strips, testimonials, FAQ, opening hours, booking calls-to-action, an Instagram wall and retail sections.

Several page templates come ready to use — services, a second discipline, team, gallery, booking, about, contact, careers, memberships, gift cards and support. To use one, create the page under Online Store → Pages and pick the matching template in the Theme template box on the right.

The template list only shows templates from your published theme. If you are still previewing Nocturne, publish it first, then assign templates to your pages.

Products, cart & filters

Nocturne is a full Shopify storefront: product pages with variants, video and 3D, a cart as a drawer or a page, gift cards, subscriptions and every accelerated checkout button. Add products under Products, then turn on the retail sections you want and set What this site shows to Services and products.

Filtering and sorting on collection and search pages is built in. The filters themselves come from Shopify, not from the theme: install Shopify's own free Search & Discovery app and configure them there. Without it you get availability and price only.

The cart behaves as a drawer by default. Switch it to a full page under Theme settings → Cart if you prefer.

Languages

The theme ships fully translated in English and Dutch — storefront text and every setting label. Add languages under Online Store → Languages and translate your own content with Shopify's free Translate & Adapt app. A language and country selector appears in the footer automatically as soon as your store publishes more than one.

Demo content

Nocturne looks finished on an empty store: every section carries placeholder photography and demo cards, so you can judge the layout before you have content. Replace the placeholders through each section's image picker.

If you would rather start from the demo store's products, collections, pages and menus, your download includes a demo-content folder with a free import pack and step-by-step instructions. It uses Shopify's own importer — no paid app.

Updating the theme

When a new version is released, Shopify offers it under Online Store → Themes. Updates keep your settings and content. They cannot keep changes made to the theme's code — one more reason to make those in a duplicate.

Before you publish an update, preview it and check the pages you have customised most. Keep the previous version unpublished for a week rather than deleting it, so you can put it back in one click.

FAQ

Do I need a booking app to use this theme?

No. The theme works with a booking app, with a link to any booking provider, or with nothing but a phone number. It never depends on one particular app.

Why do I only see two filters on my collection page?

Filters come from Shopify, not from the theme. Install Shopify's free Search & Discovery app and add the filters you want there; they appear on your storefront immediately.

Can I sell products and still look like a studio?

That is what What this site shows is for. On Services only the header drops its shop icons while your products, collections and checkout keep working. Switch to Services and products when you want the shop out front.

My page template isn't in the dropdown.

Shopify only lists templates from the theme that is currently published. Publish Nocturne first, then assign the template to your page.

Will an update overwrite my work?

Your settings, content and images are kept. Changes made directly to the theme's code are not — make those in a duplicate of the theme.

What does support cover?

Bugs in the theme, and questions about how to use it. It does not cover custom code, third-party apps, or design work on your store — for those, a Shopify Partner is the right help. See the support page.
