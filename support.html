Support

Found a bug, or stuck on a setting? Send it here. You get an automatic confirmation straight away, and a real answer from a person within two working days.

The reply goes here. Check that it is one you read.
Your .myshopify.com address, or your own domain.
Optional. A short line helps us route it faster.
What you expected, what happened instead, and where — which page, which device. The more precise, the faster this gets fixed.
Optional. One image or PDF, up to 10 MB. A screenshot answers most questions on its own.

What support covers

Bugs in the theme, and questions about how to use what it ships with. That includes:

  • something in the theme not working as documented;
  • help finding or understanding a setting;
  • problems that appear after a theme update.

It does not cover custom code, third-party apps, or design work on your store:

  • changes the settings cannot make;
  • fixing a theme whose code has been edited;
  • configuring or debugging an app you have installed.

For those, hire a Shopify Partner.

Duplicate your theme before editing any code. Code changes are lost when you install a theme update, and a theme with edited code cannot be supported.

Getting a faster answer

Most of what slows a reply down is missing detail. Before you send:

  • Add the address of the page where it happens.
  • Say whether it is on a phone, a tablet or a desktop, and in which browser.
  • Attach a screenshot — it usually replaces three messages.
  • Check the FAQ; the two most common questions are answered there.
